Common questions

How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?
Open Lumen → Settings → Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Lumen → Cancel.

My dream didn't save / interpretation failed.
Check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, email us with the date of the dream and we'll help.

Voice recording isn't working.
Make sure Lumen has microphone permission: iOS Settings → Lumen → Microphone.

How do I delete my account?
Open Lumen → Settings → Delete Account. This wipes your dreams and credentials from this device.

We're here to help you make Lumen feel like home for your dreams. Browse common questions below, or reach out anytime.

Getting started

Lumen guides you through a short onboarding to personalize your dream profile. When you're ready, tap the glowing orb on the Today screen to capture your first dream — by voice or by typing.

Recording dreams

The orb opens a capture sheet with live transcription. You can also type, mark a dream as lucid, and add a mood tag. Dreams are stored privately on your device.

Lumen Pro

Pro unlocks AI-powered dream interpretation, unlimited entries, and deeper pattern insights.

  • Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Tap Restore Purchases in Lumen Settings if you've switched devices.

Privacy

Your dreams live locally on your device in encrypted storage. Lumen never shares them. Sign-in providers (Apple, Google) are used only to identify your account.

Troubleshooting

  • Voice not transcribing? Enable Speech Recognition + Microphone in iOS Settings → Lumen.
  • Missing dreams? Dreams are tied to your device. Re-signing in restores onboarding state but not local entries.
  • Notifications silent? Check Settings → Notifications → Lumen.
